“Entiéndeme Tú a Mi” - 2023-2025

Entiéndeme tú a mí is a performative work that challenges adults—parents, caregivers, and educators—to confront what they do not understand about young people, exposing how failed communication stems from a lack of emotional education and pedagogical awareness. The piece creates an intimate space where empathy, respect, listening, acceptance, and unconditional love become urgent tools for coexistence, proposing dialogue as a means to access what lies beneath behavior: emotions, needs, and unspoken conflicts. Addressing mental health, sexuality, academic performance, technology, relationships, independence, and responsibility as interconnected experiences rather than isolated problems, the work offers adults a way to step back from control and allow children, adolescents, and young adults to be themselves, suggesting that mutual understanding is not only a social necessity but a fundamental act of care for collective mental well-being.

“El Jardín Mekánico” - December 20th, 2025

In December 2025, Viridiana showcased her work at "El Jardín Mekánico," a vibrant cultural takeover held at Espacio Colectivo Vía Láctea in Zaragoza This "Anti-Xmas Market Edition"—a collaboration between La Meka Kollective and La Fleur de L’Homme—was conceived as a creative sanctuary to challenge traditional holiday commercialism. Within this space dedicated to independent art, music, and community, Viridiana presented her latest pieces, contributing to an atmosphere that prioritized authentic expression over mass consumption. The event served as a dynamic platform for her to connect with the local creative scene, blending her artistic vision with the underground and independent spirit of the collective.

“Garra Out of Office” - September 19th, 2025

In September, Viridiana participated in a collective exhibition hosted by Garra Collective at the ESDA (Escuela Superior de Diseño de Aragón). As part of the "Garra Out of Office" event, she joined a curated group of young Aragonese artists to showcase their work in the school's main exhibition hall. The event kicked off on September 19th with an official inauguration, highlighting the fresh perspectives of emerging local talent. beyond the gallery walls, the initiative transformed the ESDA terrace into a creative hub featuring over 20 local projects spanning illustration, fashion, jewelry, and self-publishing. This exhibition allowed Viridiana to integrate her artistic practice into a dynamic multidisciplinary environment, elebrating the vibrant cultural and design scene of Zaragoza
