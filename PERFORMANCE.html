Performance

“Poor Viridiana… Whatever Happened to Her?” - 2025

This performance explores the decadence of celebrity and the dehumanization of the public figure through a stripped-down Viridiana, divested of her former glory. Naked beneath a fur coat and wearing grotesque makeup, she inhabits a precarious cardboard structure that symbolizes the collapse of her status. The space is infested with the memory of her past: press clippings from her “golden age,” now crumpled, aged, and stained with a visceral substance resembling vomit that spills from her own mouth, representing the physical and emotional rejection of her own history. Under the constant harassment of a camera flash that photographs her like a wounded animal, Viridiana oscillates within an endless cycle of vulnerability and farce, attempting to reclaim fleeting moments of beauty through automatic poses that quickly disintegrate into gestures of terror and weakness under the audience’s gaze. This piece functions as a raw critique of social voyeurism and the fragility of success, in which the artist becomes trapped in a loop of degradation, where confidence is merely the prelude to yet another fall.
